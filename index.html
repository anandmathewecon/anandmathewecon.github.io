Research

My job market paper, with Sankalp Mathur of CAFRAL, follows more than 200,000 Indian households through weather shocks and the 2018 collapse in non-bank lending, using household survey data matched to credit bureau records. When the monsoon fails, households keep total spending nearly flat and change what they buy. Where the local lender withdrew, the cuts reach further, into health, education and food staples.

I also work on drug policy and education in the United States, using Washington State's lottery for cannabis retail licenses to estimate how a nearby dispensary affects whether college students return for a second year. Papers and abstracts
